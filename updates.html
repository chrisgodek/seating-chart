Seating Chart: What's New

Open the Seating Chart

October 22, 2026

  • Edit a student's name right in the roster with the pencil button, or by double-clicking it.
  • New Names setting: show full names or first names only. If two students share a first name, they keep their full names.
  • Copy for Slides and Download Slide (PNG) make a 16:9 image of the chart, ready to paste into Google Slides. Names are sized to fill each seat.
  • New slide option, Slide: room + Find your seat list, which puts an alphabetical list of students beside the room so everyone can find their table quickly.
  • Phone hotel numbers are bigger on slides and also appear in the Find your seat list.
  • New View setting: student view (whiteboard at the top), teacher view (turned around 180°), or flipped left ↔ right.
  • Bigger table numbers and seat markers on slide images, and slide headings no longer overlap the name list.

August 2026: First version

  • Import a class roster from a CSV or text file, or paste in names.
  • Set up the room as groups of tables with rows and columns.
  • Randomize seats, with rules for students who must sit together or should be kept apart, and preferred min/max students per table.
  • Drag and drop students to adjust seats by hand.
  • Separate rosters and charts for each class period, saved charts, and printing (including all periods at once).
